Consolidated on 2026-09-12 from the [handoff](../history/handoff-2026-09-12.md), reports, and local artifacts. Historical files remain preserved. This catalog gives priority to explicit corrections and raw data when a summary conflicts with them.

**Verified** means observed within the stated scope; it is not a promise for every workload or model. **Hypothesis** means an explanation that has not yet been isolated. **Limitation** records what a measurement cannot establish. **Retracted** identifies a superseded conclusion. Server state and decisions below are the latest available records, without a new query to the machine.

## Gaps that remain open

1. **Long context:** isolate the cause of the observed limit without confusing the VRAM budget with the prompt ceiling. Original server logs are quoted/transcribed in the report; they were not reread on the machine during this documentation work.
2. **Evaluators:** the language detector accepts English as `tie`; the promise regex misses an actual wording; the spill check does not see the speculative target. Documented without silently changing scoring criteria or historical data.
3. **Generalization:** broaden question diversity and account for grouping by question in the model decision. Fixed seeds did not guarantee repeatability across sessions in the recorded data.
4. **Operations:** the effect of `keep_alive=5m`, simultaneous load on both resident models, and concurrency on `qwen3.5:9b` and the coder have not been measured. Phase 6 does not justify extending its percentages to these scenarios.
5. **Coding:** Phase 5 remains blocked; customer support and tools do not replace a coding goldset with validated execution.
6. **PT→EN:** dedicated documentation has been created; the cause is not isolated, the 264-byte excerpt is unconfirmed, and the handoff's control group was corrected against raw data. Communication to the owner and product changes did not occur during this documentation work.
7. **Missing history:** the previous session's entire JSONL was not reconstructed. Details without an accessible source remain missing; they are not filled in by assumption.
